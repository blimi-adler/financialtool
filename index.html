What Each Metric Means

COGS %Direct cost to deliver the product or service (materials, direct labor, subcontractors) divided by sales. Lower is better.
Gross Margin %What is left after COGS (100% minus COGS %). This is what pays for overhead and profit.
Overhead %Fixed operating costs (rent, admin payroll, software, insurance, marketing) divided by sales. Lower is better.
Net Profit %What the business keeps after all costs, before owner distributions. Gross margin % minus overhead %.
