NFPA 13 Quick Reference

ClassMax areaMax spacingMax wall
Auto-placement uses the standard NFPA 13 bay-spacing method (equal bays sized to the smaller of max area or max spacing, heads centered in each bay). It fills the bounding shape of each drawn area and drops any grid point that falls outside the outline you drew — irregular or L-shaped rooms may need a manual head added to patch a corner. Values are standard-spray upright/pendent defaults; obstructions, extended-coverage heads, storage occupancies, and NYS/local AHJ amendments can change these numbers. Confirm the currently adopted code edition and have the final layout reviewed and stamped by the engineer of record before submittal.
